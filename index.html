Intro to CapCut

Have you ever scroll on Tiktok, and see those really cool edits.
Then you thought to yourself "Man, I really want to make those edits by myself"
Well lucky you, because this website would tell you everything about Capcut!! I mean literally, I will shove every bit of information about capcut into your brain. Starting with it's history.

Capcut was created in May 2019, by JianYing (publish by Byte). It purpose was to create an easy, fast, simple free video editor, so everyone could create their own movie without needing to pay for expensive software.
